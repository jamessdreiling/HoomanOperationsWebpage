The Challenge

You're the Whole Back Office

If you own a business with few or no employees, the admin lands on you. Sending invoices. Chasing payments. Answering the same intake questions. Copying information from one app to another. Remembering to follow up.

Most of it gets done late at night or on weekends. And some of it doesn't get done at all: a follow-up you forgot, an invoice that sat in drafts, a form nobody saw.

That's where I come in.

I find the tasks that keep slipping and set them up to run on their own, using tools you probably already pay for. You stay in control. You just stop being the only thing holding it all together.
